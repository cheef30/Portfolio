Web design for businesses in Belgrade

I build websites and web apps for Belgrade businesses, from travel agencies to event venues. You talk directly to the person building your site, and you know the price and timeline before we start.

Get a quote

No agency between you and your website

Belgrade has plenty of agencies and even more offers. The difference with me is simple: I do everything myself, from the first call and the design to the code and the maintenance. You don’t pass your wishes through a middleman, and you don’t pay for a team you don’t need.

I’m based in Obrenovac, so meeting in person in Belgrade is easy. After the first meeting, we sort most things out by phone, Viber or video call.

A Belgrade example: the Na Izlet travel agency

Na Izlet is a Belgrade travel agency that runs day trips and tours across Europe. Its old WordPress site was slow on phones and hard to keep up to date with new departures. I built the new site from scratch in Next.js, with an admin panel where the agency adds trips, dates and prices on its own, a departure calendar and a booking system with a bus seat map.

I’m also currently building a site for Mister S Tailored Events, an event venue in central Belgrade.

What I can build for your business

  • A business website: a presentation site or landing page, with call, Viber, WhatsApp, Google Maps and a contact form
  • A web application: bookings, calendars, an admin panel, user accounts and a database
  • 3D views and animation: for property, products and brands
  • Maintenance: text, image and price updates, fixes and speed checks

Websites in Serbian and English

Many Belgrade businesses also work with foreigners: agencies, accommodation, event venues. I built bilingual sites for the Leto u Turskoj agency and for Mister S Tailored Events. Each language should have its own address, so Google can show the Serbian version to people searching in Serbian and the English version to people searching in English.

A fast site, because people open it on their phone

Most visitors open your site for the first time on a phone, often on the bus or during a break. So I prepare images in modern formats (AVIF and WebP), keep fonts and scripts on the site itself and check the speed before handover. The Leto u Turskoj site has a Lighthouse SEO score of 100 on mobile.

How we work together

  1. Conversation Free

    A short call or a coffee in Belgrade or Obrenovac. You tell me what your business does and what you expect from the site, and I send you a proposal with a price and a timeline.

  2. Design About a week

    I put together the layout and the look and send you a link to check it in your browser. You tell me what you don’t like, I fix it, and only then do I start building.

  3. Build 1 to 3 weeks

    I code the site, add your copy and photos and test it on a phone, a tablet and a desktop.

  4. Launch One day

    I connect the domain, submit the site to Google and give you all the logins.
