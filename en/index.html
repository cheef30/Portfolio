01 / 05

Modus Gradnja

Residential property developer

Year
2026
Role
Design, development and maintenance

The company sells apartments from its own developments and needed buyers to find their apartment on their own, without PDFs going back and forth. I built the site around a 3D model of the building: buyers rotate it, pick a floor and an apartment, and instantly see the layout, floor area and price.

  • 3D model generated in code with Three.js, no heavy 3D files
  • 2D floor plan synced with the 3D view, both ways
  • 70 apartments, each with its own page, sales sheet and enquiry form
  • Monthly maintenance: I keep prices and availability up to date
  • Three.js

  • CSS
  • JSON-LD

How I work

This is what working together looks like, from the first call to the site on your domain.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    Free

    A short call or a coffee in Obrenovac or Belgrade. You tell me what your business does and what you expect from the site, and afterwards I send you a proposal with a price and a timeline.

  2. 02

    Design

    About a week

    I put together the layout and the look and send you a link to check it in your browser. You tell me what you don’t like, I fix it, and only then do I start building.

  3. 03

    Build

    1 to 3 weeks

    I code the site, add your copy and photos and test it on a phone, a tablet and a desktop.

  4. 04

    Launch

    One day

    I connect the domain, submit the site to Google and give you all the logins. If something needs changing later, I’m around.

The call is free and doesn’t commit you to anything.
